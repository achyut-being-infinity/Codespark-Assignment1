Tech Detective Assignments

Research one mechanism. Present it in 4–5 minutes.
Sitara Rebba
How do smartwatches count your steps?
Focus only on: the accelerometer — the tiny sensor that senses movement and turns it into a step count.
  • What is an accelerometer and where is it inside the watch?
  • How does shaking or moving become a "step" in the count?
  • Can you find one real example of it being wrong (false step)?
Sai Gautam
How does Google Lens identify objects?
Focus only on: how the app matches your photo against millions of trained images to guess what it is.
  • What happens in the second right after you take the photo?
  • What does "trained on millions of images" actually mean?
  • Try it yourself on 2 objects — did it get both right?
Aarna Bartaria
How do online multiplayer games connect players?
Focus only on: the client-server idea — how a central server lets two far-apart players see the same game.
  • What is a "server" in simple words?
  • Why do both players need to talk to the same server?
  • Name one game you play and how many players connect to it.
Vansh Wadhwa
How does Alexa understand your voice?
Focus only on: the wake word ("Alexa") and how spoken words get converted into text.
  • What is a "wake word" and why is it always listening for just that?
  • How does sound become text a computer can read?
  • What happens if it mishears a word?
Vedansh Vuppala
How does Face Unlock recognize you?
Focus only on: the infrared dot map — how the phone scans the shape of your face to check it's really you.
  • What are the tiny infrared dots doing on your face?
  • Why can't a photo of you usually unlock the phone?
  • What's one situation where Face Unlock might fail?

How does NASA communicate with spacecraft?
Focus only on: the Deep Space Network — the giant Earth antennas that send and receive signals to spacecraft.
  • What is the Deep Space Network and where are its antennas?
  • Why does a message take minutes (or hours) to reach a spacecraft?
  • Name one spacecraft this network talks to today.
